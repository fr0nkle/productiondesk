Production Desk

Local film scheduling software tool made by some friends at Squid Productions to help make scheduling and script breakdowns easier.

Break down your script, arrange your shooting days, and keep your shot list together. Keep It Simple Stupid.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

Mac: Apple Silicon, macOS 12 or later. Windows: Windows 10/11, 64-bit (x64). One complete offline .exe installer; no separate runtime downloads.

These personal builds are not Apple-notarized or Windows code-signed. macOS may require first-launch approval; Windows may show a SmartScreen prompt.

The plan before the shoot.

  • Import PDF, Final Draft, Fountain, or text scripts and break them into scenes.
  • Tag cast, props, costumes, and other production elements alongside your script.
  • Arrange a stripboard into shooting days and compare schedule scenarios.
  • Build scene-linked shot lists with camera details, priorities, and time estimates.
  • Print schedules and breakdowns, export shot lists, and save portable production backups.

Projects stay on your device. No account or subscription.
