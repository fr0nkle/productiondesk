Production Desk

Local film scheduling software tool made by some friends at Squid Productions to help make scheduling and script breakdowns easier.

Download Production Desk for Mac

For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 12 or later. Unzip the download and move Production Desk.app into your Applications folder. Existing projects are preserved when replacing the app.

Already installed? Choose Production Desk → Check for Updates… in the app.

This personal build is not Apple-notarized. macOS may require first-launch approval.
